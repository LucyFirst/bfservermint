[ ] Story quest item drops (added 28 Oct 2026)
    Only 113 of the 1,173 story quests have the original game's drop rates (datamine). About 690 are
    built on recordings, almost all of them ONE recorded run, and the server replays that run's chests
    as if they were the drop table - e.g. the last Kagan Desert quest gives ~0.5 Red Claw per run
    because one recorded run found one (Phoenix of the Volcano, which has real rates: ~0.1).
    Plan: for quests with few recorded runs, show recorded chests less often (about 1/3 with one run)
    and draw about half the chests from the area's wiki item list, at rates based on the 113
    datamined quests. Many story quests will give fewer items after this - tell players.
