Story areas

Ticked areas are closed: they disappear from the quest map and their quests can't be started.

Vortex banners

Each banner and the quests behind it, grouped by Vortex tab. Close banners, change their picture, move them to another tab, or make your own banner from copies of existing quests. Picture and banner changes reach players the next time they open the game.

Game areas

Switch parts of the game off for every player. These are the game's own switches, so a switched-off area cannot be opened - players do not get sent back to the home screen. Players see changes after fully closing and reopening the game.

Raid Classes open

Raid quests above this Raid Class are not offered, and nobody's Raid Class goes past it (clearing the class-up quest of the top open class does nothing until you open the next one).

Trials

The Summoners' Research Lab trials, in the order they came out. A trial that is switched off is not in the game's trial list at all, and cannot be started. When a trial is off, the next one unlocks with whatever opened the switched-off one. Every trial gives its rewards once per account, on the first clear only. Fighting it again gives no exp, zel, drops, tasks or gifts. Players see on/off changes after logging in again and fully restarting the game.

Summons

Every summon the game knows. The game's Summon screen is one list you swipe through, in this order. Change what each summon costs, which units it gives and how often, and its picture. Players see changes the next time they open the Summon screen.

Honor Summon

The summon paid for with honor points from helpers and friends.

Closed units no longer come from summons or quest drops, and no unit can evolve into them (the game shows the unit before as fully evolved). Units players already own stay. Tap units to select them.

Banner picture

Pick an image - it is fitted to the game's banner size (640 x 224).

Drop rates

Drop rates for this banner's quests only. They work on top of the overall rates (Rates tab), so with overall Unit drop x2 and x1.5 here, units drop x3 as often. 1 = normal, 0 = none.

The chance a unit is caught each time it is beaten, in normal battles and the boss battle. Empty = the usual rate. 100 = always caught. The unit drop multipliers above still apply on top.

Reward boosts

Multiply the EXP, Zel or Karma a Vortex banner's quests give. The game shows it on the banner (for example "XP Received x2"), and zel / karma dropped in battle are multiplied too. The game shows one boost per banner, so a banner holds one boost at a time. This is on top of the overall rates. Whole numbers only (2-99): the game's banner number has no decimal point.

New Vortex banner

News

The page players see from Home > Info, Menu > News and News on the title screen. One banner picture at the top, then your posts (newest first - use the arrows to reorder). In the text: an empty line starts a new paragraph, lines starting with "- " become a list, and **word** is bold.

Sphere House: event spheres

The Sphere House lists these spheres from level 1, but their materials came from events, Raid, Guild, Frontier Hunter and so on. Switched-off spheres are hidden from every player's Sphere List and cannot be crafted. Switch a group on when you bring in its event. The Sphere House's own level-up spheres and the story / town harvest ones are always on. "Drops now" = the server already gives out everything the sphere needs.

Rates

1 = normal. 2 = double, 0.5 = half. Exp, zel and karma are the quest clear rewards; drop and chest rates change how often units drop and chests appear.

Unit box

How many units every player can hold before buying more space with gems. The game starts everyone at 300. Space a player bought with gems is kept on top of this. Players see the new size the next time they open the game.

Fusion success

Great and Super Success when fusing units. Tick a boost to use your own chance and exp multiplier for it; unticked it stays normal (Great 12% x1.5, Super 3% x2). The label under the Unit screen's Fusion button is your choice below ("Super Success Rate Up" needs app v12 or newer). The game shows the multiplier after a fusion ("Super Success! 2x XP!") - players see a new multiplier after fully closing and reopening the game.

Unit sell prices

Zel a player gets for selling one of these units. The game shows the new price on the sell screen too. Players see changes after fully closing and reopening the game.
